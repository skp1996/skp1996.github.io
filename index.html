CyberShield Studio

CyberShield Studio is the private publishing tool behind the Cybershield Academy YouTube channels, which teach everyday cyber safety in English and Hindi: spotting phishing, creating strong passwords, staying safe on public Wi-Fi, and more.

What the app does

  • Uploads the channel owner's own videos to their own YouTube channels and schedules them.
  • Adds subtitles, thumbnails, playlist entries and chapter lists to those videos.
  • Updates the channels' own description, keywords and banner.

It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to other users.
